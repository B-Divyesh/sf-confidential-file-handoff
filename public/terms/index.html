Using the tool

Terms

This local utility creates protected ZIP files and handoff sheets. Use it only for files you may handle.

Limits and compatibility

The tool does not hide ZIP entry names or verify a recipient. You enter the name and choose both routes yourself.

If opening fails, the handoff sheet names 7-Zip, Keka, and PeaZip. It asks the recipient to report their device and app.

Pro purchase

Pro costs US $9 once and adds a personal note to the handoff sheet.

Sociobot/Dodo handles payment. A revoked license turns off the personal-note field.

Creating the ZIP, handoff sheet, handoff log, and exports remains free.

Availability

The app is provided as-is, without warranty. Test a sample file when the handoff matters.
