Privacy

Confidential File Handoff is designed to work on your device. It does not upload your selected files, ZIP password, file names, or recipient sheet to our servers.

What stays on your device

When you prepare a handoff, your browser processes the selected files and creates the encrypted ZIP locally. The app may save a local handoff checklist in your browser’s IndexedDB: recipient first name, date created, selected delivery route, selected password route, and optional sent/opened timestamps. It never stores the files, file names, or password in that checklist.

The encrypted ZIP itself does expose its entry names before the password is entered. Rename files before adding them if their names reveal sensitive information.

Your choices

You can export the local checklist as JSON, import a prior export, or delete individual entries. Imports accept only the checklist fields described above. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes the local checklist. A downloaded ZIP and handoff sheet are controlled by your browser’s download settings.

Pro licenses

If you buy Pro or restore a license, the license token is stored in this browser and checked with Sociobot’s billing API no more than daily while online. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

Contact

For product privacy questions, contact the operator through the Sociobot site. Do not send confidential documents in a support request.
